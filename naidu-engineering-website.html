Raj Naidu

Mutual preference, a commitment to quality, and a high standard of ethics — on every project, at every scale.

Raj Naidu is the CEO and Principal Engineer of Naidu Engineering LLC, with more than 25 years of experience spanning small municipal drainage work to mega infrastructure programs exceeding $1 billion — including interstate highways, airports, and heavy industrial complexes across Florida, Virginia, Maryland, Idaho, Oregon, Colorado, California, and Washington.

Before founding the firm in 2017, Raj led business development, client relationships, and project controls for several multinational engineering corporations, and delivered construction engineering and management projects in India, the UAE, and the Republic of Malawi.

What we design

Full-service civil engineering, built around the calculation.

Modeling, drainage design, plans and specifications, permitting, and construction support — delivered for public agencies and private industry across every phase of a project.

  • Modeling

    Hydrology & hydraulic modeling

    Simulation and design using MGSFlood, WWHM, Stormshed, HEC-RAS, HY-8, Culvert Master, SWMM, HI-RUN, CORMIX, Autodesk SSA/StormCAD, Bentley Inroads H&H, and MODRET.

  • Water Quality

    Conveyance & flow control BMPs

    Conveyance, water quality, and water quantity / flow-control best management practice design for public and private sites.

  • Documentation

    Reports & technical memoranda

    Hydraulic reports, technical memoranda, information reports, and stormwater site plans built to agency standard.

  • GSI / LID

    Green stormwater infrastructure

    Low Impact Development and green infrastructure strategies integrated into roadway and site drainage design.

  • Roadway

    Drainage for highways & interstates

    Roadway and interstate highway drainage design, from conceptual layout through final plans.

  • Compliance

    Erosion, sediment & NPDES-CGP

    Erosion and sediment control, construction stormwater management, and NPDES Construction General Permit compliance.

  • Delivery

    Plans, specs & estimates

    AutoCAD Civil 3D and Bentley MicroStation/Inroads plans, specifications, special provisions, and estimates meeting WSDOT, Sound Transit, Seattle, Bothell, and Tacoma standards.

  • QA / QC

    Peer review & value engineering

    Design peer review, value engineering, and quality control / quality assurance across project delivery methods.

  • Delivery Methods

    Program & construction management

    Project and program management, scheduling, project controls, and construction engineering services across Design-Bid-Build, P3, and Design-Build.
